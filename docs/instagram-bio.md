## 0. The recommendation (copy-paste this)

**Name field** (30 char max — this is the searchable one):

```
conagentes | Agente IA hoteles
```

**Bio** (134 chars of 150):

```
Más ingreso. Menos trabajo.
Cierra la reserva directa y sube el valor de cada estadía. WhatsApp · 24/7 · 32 idiomas.
👇 Pruébelo gratis
```

**Category:** `Software`  ·  **Buttons:** WhatsApp (primary) + Email
**Link:** `conagentes.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio`

Why this one: it is the site's own H1 (`Más ingreso. Menos trabajo.`), so the ad → profile → landing page reads as one promise instead of three. Line 1 is the dream outcome, line 2 is *how* plus three specifics that make it believable, line 3 is one CTA. Nothing else competes.

## 1. Why the profile is structured this way

| Principle | Who says it | What it changed here |
|---|---|---|
| **The name field is search real estate, not a place for your brand name twice.** Instagram indexes *username + name field*, not bio text. | Later / Hootsuite IG-SEO research, Latasha James | `conagentes` alone is unsearchable by a hotelier who doesn't know us. `conagentes \| Agente IA hoteles` ranks for «agente IA hoteles», «IA hoteles» — exactly 30 chars. |
| **The grunt test:** a stranger must say what you offer, how it helps, and what to do next — in 5 seconds. | Donald Miller, *StoryBrand* | Offer = agente IA. Help = más reservas directas, mayor valor por estadía. Next = tap the link. All three visible without "ver más". |
| **Name the category the buyer would search for; don't invent one.** | April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome* | «Agente IA para hoteles» lives in the name field. We do **not** spend bio characters re-declaring it. |
| **Specificity beats adjectives.** Numbers survive skepticism; "solución integral" doesn't. | Harry Dry, *Marketing Examples* | `24/7 · 32 idiomas` instead of «atención multilingüe permanente». |
| **Dream outcome first, mechanism second.** Lead with what they get, not what it is. | Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers* | `Más ingreso. Menos trabajo.` is the whole value equation in 27 characters — more of the good, less of the effort. |
| **Hard ROI, not soft ROI.** Revenue framing commands price; efficiency framing caps it. | Bessemer AI Pricing Playbook; Kyle Poyar | Bio says *reserva directa* and *valor de la estadía* — revenue verbs. It never says "ahorra tiempo" or "reduce costos". |
| **One CTA.** Two CTAs in a bio halve both. | Peep Laja / CXL, standard CRO | Only `👇 Pruébelo gratis`. The WhatsApp button carries the second path without spending characters. |
| **Emoji as bullets, not decoration.** | Jenna Kutcher, Later | Exactly one 👇, pointing at the link. No emoji soup, no flag, no sparkles. |
| **Trust cues near the ask.** | Cialdini | `Factura DIAN` / `PMS gratis` are strong, but they belong in Highlights and the pinned post — not in 150 shared characters. Variant C tests putting them in the bio. |

**Bans carried over from the positioning brief (§3.1, §3.4):** no «chatbot», no «asistente virtual», no «automatización de mensajes», no «solución integral». Usted register. Outcome before the word "IA". OTAs are never the villain.

## 2. The four variants worth testing

Run one for 2–3 weeks, watch profile-visits → link-taps, then swap. Character counts are UTF-16 (what Instagram actually enforces); all four fit under 150.

**A — Outcome / site-matched (recommended, 134)**
```
Más ingreso. Menos trabajo.
Cierra la reserva directa y sube el valor de cada estadía. WhatsApp · 24/7 · 32 idiomas.
👇 Pruébelo gratis
```
Strongest for cold traffic arriving from ads, because it repeats the landing page word for word.

**B — Hire-a-worker frame (143)**
```
El vendedor que su hotel nunca pudo contratar.
Atiende, cotiza y cierra la reserva mientras usted duerme. 24/7 · 32 idiomas.
👇 Pruébelo gratis
```
The tool→worker pivot (brief §1) stated outright. Best if the audience is owners who have already rejected a cheap chatbot.

**C — Bandwidth gap + local trust (148)**
```
Su equipo no da abasto. Su agente IA sí.
Reserva directa, upsell y huéspedes recuperados — por WhatsApp, 24/7.
Factura DIAN · PMS gratis
👇 Pruébelo
```
Carries the two uncontested claims (brief §4). Denser — worse for a 5-second scan, better for a warm audience comparing us to Alzio/Visito.

**D — Loss aversion, sourced (146)**
```
El 40% de los mensajes de madrugada se queda sin respuesta.
El nuestro los convierte en reservas. WhatsApp · 24/7 · 32 idiomas.
👇 Pruébelo gratis
```
Highest-stopping-power opener. **Only ship this if the 40% after-hours figure is cited in the pinned post** (brief §4 benchmark) — an uncited statistic in a bio reads as invented.

## 3. The rest of the profile (the bio is a third of the job)

- **Profile photo:** the logo mark on the dark canvas, legible at 32 px. Not the wordmark — it turns to mud at thumbnail size.
- **Action buttons:** WhatsApp first. The product *is* WhatsApp; making the prospect leave the app to find the product is a self-inflicted wound.
- **Link:** one destination, UTM-tagged. Skip link-in-bio aggregators — an extra tap costs more than the extra options are worth while there is one offer.
- **Highlights** (4, in this order, covers matching the site's neon-accent-on-dark): `Cómo vende` · `Hoteles` · `DIAN y TRA` · `Preguntas`. These are where the compliance and PMS proof live.
- **Pinned posts** (3): the 30-second demo of the agent closing a reservation · one named, quantified hotel reference (blocked on brief §9 decision 6) · the DIAN/TRA compliance explainer.
- **Bio ≠ pitch deck.** Everything the bio couldn't hold belongs in a Highlight, and every Highlight should end on the same CTA as the bio.
